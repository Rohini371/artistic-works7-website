Turn your celebration into a living artwork

Our live painting experiences are designed to feel elegant, relaxed and memorable from the very first brushstroke.

Wedding live painting is a graceful way to document your love story in real time. While your guests enjoy the moment, an artist creates a custom painting inspired by your theme, venue and the emotion of the day.

Whether you’re planning a classic Indian wedding, a modern rooftop reception or an intimate celebration, the experience can be tailored to match your aesthetic and pace.
